Raw footage in.
A directed cut out.

Upload a handful of clips, describe the vibe if you want one, and an LLM plans the edit: pacing, transitions, color, music ducked under dialogue. The renderer then builds it deterministically.

  1. 1

    Upload footage

    1–5 clips, video or audio, at least one has to be video.

  2. 2

    Describe the cut

    Optional: what to open on, the vibe, what to use as music. Skip it and it finds the strongest moments on its own.

  3. 3

    Get the edit back

    A graded, subtitled cut lands a few minutes later. Review the edit plan clip by clip, tweak a timing, rerender.

what it can do

Cuts & transitions

Hard cuts, crossfades, fades, paced to the footage.

Color

Cinematic teal-orange, vivid, black & white.

Subtitles

Word-accurate captions, burned in.

Music

Auto-ducked under dialogue.

Aspect

16:9, 9:16, 1:1.
